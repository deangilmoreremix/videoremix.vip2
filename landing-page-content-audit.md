## 1. Current State Summary

The live landing page positions VideoRemix.vip almost exclusively as an **"AI Marketing Personalization Platform"**:

- **Page title:** `VideoRemix.vip - AI Marketing Platform + 100+ Business Apps`
- **H1:** *"Drive your marketing Engagement with AI personalization."*
- **Core narrative:** Generic marketing → personalized marketing → higher conversions.
- **Key sections:**
  - Hero with "12,467+ marketing teams" trust claim
  - "The Marketing Crisis" (6 problem cards)
  - "Introducing VideoRemix.vip" solution section
  - Features grid heavily slanted toward video/personalization marketing
  - "Complete App Bundles" (12 bundles, 60–70% savings)
  - Integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Shopify, etc.)
  - Resources & Insights
  - ROI Calculator
  - Testimonials / social proof

**What this misses:** The ecosystem has expanded far beyond marketing personalization. The awesome-llm-apps catalog contributes 100+ AI apps across categories like agent skills, voice AI, RAG, coding, finance, legal, recruitment, and more. The user's additional apps cover sales funnels, CRM, screen recording, digital signatures, resume/profile generation, video editing, and referral tools.

## 3. Recommended Content Strategy

### Core Positioning Change

**From:** *"AI Marketing Personalization Platform"*  
**To:** *"Your AI App Ecosystem — Marketing, Sales, Creative, and Productivity Tools"*

The homepage should position VideoRemix.vip as a **unified hub** for 100+ AI-powered business apps, not just a marketing personalization tool. Keep the existing design structure, but rewrite copy to reflect the breadth.

### New Messaging Architecture

| Section | Current Copy Focus | Recommended Copy Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Page Title** | AI Marketing Platform + 100+ Business Apps | AI App Ecosystem — 100+ Marketing, Sales & Productivity Apps |
| **H1** | Drive your marketing Engagement with AI personalization. | Run your entire business with AI apps — marketing, sales, video, hiring, and more. |
| **Subhead** | Transform generic marketing into personalized experiences... | One platform. 100+ AI apps. From personalized content to CRM closers, video editing to screen recording, sales funnels to signature automation. |
| **Trust line** | Trusted by 12,467+ marketing teams worldwide | Trusted by 12,000+ teams across marketing, sales, and operations |
| **Problem section** | The Marketing Crisis — generic content gets ignored | The App Gap — juggling 10+ disconnected tools for sales, marketing, hiring, and content creation |
| **Solution section** | Introducing VideoRemix.vip — the world's most advanced AI marketing personalization platform | Introducing VideoRemix.vip — the unified AI app hub with 12 bundles and 100+ business-ready apps |
| **Features grid** | Personalized Video Marketing, Marketing Copy, Marketing Elements, etc. | Group by **app category** instead of marketing-only features: Content & Video, Sales & CRM, Hiring & Profiles, Productivity, Voice & Agents, Knowledge & RAG |
| **Bundles** | Sales, Lead Gen & Prospecting Bundle, etc. | Keep bundle names but update descriptions to reflect cross-category value (e.g., "Everything you need to attract leads, close deals, and onboard new hires") |
| **CTA buttons** | Get Started, Watch 60s Demo | Get Started, Explore All Apps |
| **Resources** | AI Personalization Trends, ROI Calculator Guide, 300% Engagement Lift | Add resources relevant to sales, hiring, video editing, CRM |
| **Stats / Numbers** | 350% Higher Conversions, etc. | Diversify: 100+ Apps, 12 Bundles, Multi-Category Coverage |

- `src/pages/HomePage.tsx` or similar (not present in this repo)
- Bundle/marketing JSON data files
- SEO meta tags in the app shell

If the landing page is built from a separate repo or CMS, provide this document to whoever owns that source.

1. Update `<title>` and `<meta description>` immediately.
2. Rewrite H1 + subhead on the hero.
3. Swap the "Marketing Crisis" section for "App Gap."
4. Update the 7 feature cards to category-based titles.

## 6. Open Questions

1. **Source repo for the landing page:** The `videoremix.vip2` repo contains the personalizer module and built assets, but not the main landing page source. Where is the actual homepage component maintained?
2. **App count accuracy:** The page currently claims "100+ Business Apps" in the title but "12 bundles" in the body. Should we standardize on the 100+ figure from awesome-llm-apps, or use a more conservative count?
3. **Bundle taxonomy:** The user listed ~20 apps. Should these be folded into the existing 12 bundles, or do they warrant new bundle definitions?
4. **Subdomain apps live status:** Several `ai-*.videoremix.vip` subdomains returned errors or loading screens during audit. Should the homepage link only to verified live apps?
